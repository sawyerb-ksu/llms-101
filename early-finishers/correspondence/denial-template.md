# ILL Denial Notice — Standard Wording (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

Subject: Update on your interlibrary loan request {request_id}

Dear {first_name},

Thank you for your interlibrary loan request for *{item_title}*. Unfortunately, we are unable to
fill this request.

**Reason:** {reason}

If you have questions, or would like help finding an alternative, reply to this email or contact
the Interlibrary Loan office. A librarian is also happy to help you find related sources.

Sincerely,
Interlibrary Loan Services
