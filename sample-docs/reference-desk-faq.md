# Reference Desk FAQ — Internal (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*Maintained by: Research & Instruction · Last reviewed: 2026-07-30*

Quick answers for the questions we get most. When in doubt, refer the patron to a subject
liaison rather than guessing.

## "How do I get an article the library doesn't have?"

Submit an interlibrary loan (ILL) request through the ILL portal. Articles usually arrive as a PDF
by email within 1–3 business days. Books take 5–10 business days. There is no charge for
current students, faculty, or staff.

## "Can I access databases from home?"

Yes. Any database link on the library website will prompt for university sign-in when off
campus. If a link fails, clear browser cookies and try again, then report the broken link via the
"Report a problem" form.

## "Where can I print?"

Printers are on floors 1 and 2. Black-and-white is $0.08/page, color $0.40/page, charged to the
student account. Large-format poster printing is in the Makerspace by appointment.

## "Can you help me with my citation?"

Yes, but we teach rather than format for the patron. Point them to the citation style guides and
the citation manager workshops. **Do not rely on AI tools to generate citations** — verify every
reference against the actual source.

## "Is this source peer-reviewed?"

Check the journal in Ulrichsweb (look for the referee jersey icon) or the journal's own website
for its review process. A database "peer-reviewed" filter is a good start but is not always
accurate.

## "I need help with a systematic review."

Refer to the Health Sciences or Social Sciences liaison. Systematic review consultations require
an appointment and at least two weeks' notice.

## Things we do NOT do at the desk

- Give legal or medical advice (refer to appropriate campus offices).
- Share information about what another patron has borrowed or asked about.
- Edit or proofread papers (refer to the Writing Center).
