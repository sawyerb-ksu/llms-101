# All-Staff Meeting Minutes — August 12, 2026 (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*Recorder: Jordan Ellis · Present: 23 staff · Location: Hale 301 and Zoom*

## 1. Fall semester opening

Dana Whitfield reported that the renovated second-floor study area will open August 19, one week
before classes. Twelve new group study rooms are bookable through the room reservation system.
Signage is delayed; temporary printed signs will be used until mid-September.

**Action:** Facilities liaison to confirm the signage delivery date by August 20.

## 2. Service desk hours

Discussion of combining the circulation and reference desks into a single service point during
evenings (after 7 p.m.). Several staff raised concerns about training coverage for reference
questions. The group agreed to a six-week pilot starting September 2, with a review at the
October all-staff meeting.

**Action:** Marcus Oyelaran to draft an evening service point training checklist by August 26.

## 3. Budget update

The collections budget is flat for FY27. Two database subscriptions with low usage (under 40
sessions per year) will not be renewed. Liaisons should notify affected departments by
September 15. Student worker hours are reduced by 5% across all units.

## 4. AI tools working group

Priya Natarajan presented a proposal to form a working group on AI tools in library workflows.
Scope includes an approved-tools list, guidance on patron data, and a staff workshop series.
Concern was raised about staff installing browser extensions and plugins without IT review.
The proposal was approved; the group will report back in December.

**Action:** Volunteers for the AI working group to email Priya by August 22.
