# Makerspace 3D Printing Guidelines (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*Author: Makerspace Coordinator · Updated: 2026-05-02*

## Eligibility

Current students, faculty, and staff may submit 3D print jobs after completing the online
Makerspace safety orientation (about 20 minutes). Orientation completion is valid for one
academic year.

## Submitting a job

1. Export your model as an `.stl` or `.3mf` file.
2. Upload through the Makerspace request form, choosing a filament color from the list on the form.
3. Staff review every file within two business days. You'll receive an email with an estimated
   print time and cost, and you must approve it before printing starts.

## Cost

PLA filament is $0.05 per gram, with a $1 minimum per job. Prints over 500 grams or longer than
18 hours require a consultation with Makerspace staff. Resin printing is available for faculty
research projects only.

## What we won't print

- Weapons, weapon parts, or realistic replicas.
- Items that violate copyright or trademark (for example, licensed characters for resale).
- Anything intended to contact food or skin for extended periods, as our filament is not
  certified for those uses.
- Parts that are load-bearing or safety-critical.

## Pickup

Completed prints are held at the Makerspace desk for 14 days. Unclaimed prints are recycled, and
the charge still applies. Failed prints caused by equipment issues are reprinted at no cost;
failed prints caused by file design issues are charged.

## Hours

Monday–Thursday 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday 10 a.m.–5 p.m., closed weekends.
