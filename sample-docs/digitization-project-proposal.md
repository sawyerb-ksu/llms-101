# Digitization Proposal: County Extension Bulletins, 1915–1960 (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*Submitted by: Special Collections & Digital Scholarship · Date: 2026-06-18*

## Summary

We propose digitizing approximately 1,850 agricultural extension bulletins published between
1915 and 1960 and making them full-text searchable in the institutional repository. The bulletins
are among the most requested items in Special Collections and are fragile; several have already
been withdrawn from handling.

## Scope

- **Items:** ~1,850 bulletins, 8–40 pages each (est. 29,000 pages total).
- **Condition:** About 15% have brittle paper or detached covers and need conservation review
  before scanning.
- **Copyright:** Works published by the state before 1929 are public domain. Later bulletins were
  state publications; General Counsel review is required before public release.

## Workflow

1. Condition assessment and minor conservation (Preservation unit).
2. Scanning at 400 ppi, TIFF masters, on the overhead book scanner.
3. OCR and PDF/A derivatives generated in batch.
4. Metadata created in Dublin Core, using the existing bulletin series authority records.
5. Ingest into the repository and quality review of 10% of items.

## Timeline and staffing

The project is estimated at 18 months with 0.5 FTE staff and 40 student worker hours per week.
Scanning would begin January 2027 after conservation review.

## Risks

The main risk is the student worker hour reduction announced for FY27, which may extend the
timeline to 24 months. A secondary risk is that OCR quality on older typefaces may require manual
correction for a subset of items.
