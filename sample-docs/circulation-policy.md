# Circulation Policy — Hale Library (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*Author: Access Services Committee · Effective: 2026-01-15 · Supersedes: 2023 policy*

## Who can borrow

Current students, faculty, and staff may borrow with a valid university ID. Community borrowers
may register for a Community Card ($25/year) with a photo ID and proof of Kansas residency.
Alumni Association members receive a Community Card at no charge.

## Loan periods

| Borrower type | Books | Media (DVD, audio) | Laptops | Interlibrary loan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate | 28 days | 7 days | 4 hours | Set by lending library |
| Graduate | 90 days | 7 days | 4 hours | Set by lending library |
| Faculty / Staff | Semester | 14 days | 3 days | Set by lending library |
| Community Card | 21 days | 7 days | Not eligible | Not eligible |

Books may be renewed twice online unless another patron has placed a hold. Laptops, media, and
course reserves cannot be renewed.

## Fines and replacement

The library no longer charges overdue fines on books (fine-free since fall 2024). The following
still apply:

- **Laptops:** $5 per hour overdue, capped at $100.
- **Course reserves:** $1 per hour overdue, capped at $20.
- **Lost items:** Replacement cost plus a $15 processing fee. If the item is returned within
  90 days of billing, the replacement cost is refunded; the processing fee is not.

Accounts with more than $50 in charges are blocked from borrowing until the balance is below $50.

## Holds and recalls

Patrons may place holds on checked-out items. Faculty may recall an item for course use; the
current borrower then has 7 days to return it, regardless of original due date.

## Privacy

Circulation records are confidential. Staff may not disclose what a patron has borrowed to anyone
other than the patron, except as required by law and approved by the Dean's office. Borrowing
history is deleted when an item is returned without charges.

## Questions

Contact the Circulation Desk, first floor, or circ@example.edu.
